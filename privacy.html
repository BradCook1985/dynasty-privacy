This page describes how Dynasty Hockey handles data based on the implemented product inventory. It is not legal advice.

What we collect

  • Gameplay progress — saved dynasties, achievements, collection, preferences, and VS journal entries (device-local; may sync when signed in).
  • Account identity — public username, display name, and sign-in provider links. Email is used for authentication only and is not shown as your public username.
  • Optional analytics — anonymous product events when PostHog is configured. You can opt out in-app. Session replay is off.

What we do not collect for public display

  • OAuth access or refresh tokens (never stored in Dynasty databases or localStorage).
  • Provider legal names as your Dynasty username.
  • Precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data.

Where data lives

  • Your device — guest and signed-in progress is local-first in browser storage.
  • Cloud (when enabled) — Supabase Postgres with row-level security for account sync.
  • Credential bridge — Firebase may be used only to obtain sign-in tokens; Dynasty does not store Firebase sessions.

Retention

Guest data remains on your device until you clear site data. Signed-in cloud data is deleted when you delete your account. Hosting logs may retain standard HTTP metadata for a limited operational period.

Your choices

  • Play as Guest without an account.
  • Export local data from Support (in-app).
  • Opt out of product analytics in the Privacy panel.
  • Delete your account permanently (not deactivation).
