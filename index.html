Dynasty Privacy Policy

Dynasty Hockey

Effective Date: August 2, 2026

Dynasty Hockey (“Dynasty,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a mobile hockey dynasty draft game published by Brad Cook Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Dynasty Hockey app.

Summary: Dynasty collects your email address (including Apple’s private relay email when you choose Hide My Email), a unique account identifier, and profile information needed for account authentication and multiplayer functionality. We do not use advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Information Collected

When you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Email address — used for account authentication and account recovery. If you sign in with Apple and choose Hide My Email, Apple may provide a private relay address (for example, an @privaterelay.appleid.com address) instead of your personal email.
  • Unique account identifier — a UUID assigned to your account and, when applicable, your Apple Sign In provider identifier, used to link your account across sessions and devices.
  • Profile information — such as a display name and username, used to identify you in the app and to support multiplayer features (for example, matchmaking and friend invites).

When you use signed-in multiplayer and cloud features, gameplay-related data necessary for those features (such as match participation, competitive statistics, and saved game progress) is stored in association with your account identifier. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or browsing history.

How Information Is Used

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Authenticate you when you sign in
  • Enable multiplayer matchmaking, VS mode, and related game features
  • Sync your progress and competitive statistics when you are signed in
  • Respond to support requests and protect the security of our services

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising or behavioral profiling.

Authentication via Apple Sign In

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple authenticates you and shares limited information with our authentication provider (see Supabase below). Depending on your Apple settings, this may include your name (on first authorization only), your email address or a private relay email, and a stable Apple user identifier.

You can manage Apple Sign In permissions in your Apple ID settings. Dynasty does not receive your Apple ID password.

Supabase Authentication and Database

Dynasty uses Supabase for secure authentication and cloud database storage. When you sign in:

  • Your email address and account identifier are stored in Supabase Auth
  • Your username, display name, and gameplay data linked to your account are stored in Supabase’s database
  • Authentication tokens are handled by Supabase and your device; we do not store OAuth secrets in the app database

Supabase processes data on our behalf as a service provider. Their privacy practices are described at supabase.com/privacy.

Data Sharing

We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — such as Supabase (authentication and database hosting) and Apple (when you use Sign in with Apple), solely to operate the app
  • Other players — your public username and display name may be visible to opponents during multiplayer matches or on leaderboards
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), secure authentication flows, and access controls on cloud data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we work to safeguard your data and review our practices regularly.

User Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access or update your profile information in the app
  • Delete your account and associated cloud data through the in-app account deletion flow
  • Sign out and continue as a guest without creating an account (guest progress remains on your device)
  • Contact us with questions or requests regarding your data

To request account deletion or data assistance, use the in-app Delete Account option or email us at the address below.

Children’s Privacy

Dynasty Hockey is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the Effective Date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
