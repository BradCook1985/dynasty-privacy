For help with Dynasty Hockey

Open Dynasty Hockey and choose More → Support / Send Feedback.

Account and profile

  • Sign in from Account to sync progress when cloud services are enabled.
  • You can play as a guest without creating an account.
  • Signed-in players can export local Dynasty data from Account (Export / Download Backup).
  • Public usernames may appear in VS Challenge matchmaking and leaderboards.

Account deletion

Deleting your account is permanent — not deactivation. Use Account → Delete account in the app, or see the Delete Account page.
