Privacy Terms Support Delete Account

Permanent deletion — not deactivation. This removes your cloud account, profile, synced dynasties, achievements, collection, and settings. It cannot be undone.

Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Dynasty Hockey and sign in.
  2. Go to Account → Profile → Delete account.
  3. Confirm deletion and complete recent sign-in verification (password or provider).

What is deleted

  • Supabase authentication account and profile
  • Cloud saved dynasties, achievements, player collection, settings, sync metadata
  • Competitive rating rows tied to your user id
  • Sign in with Apple token at Apple (when server revocation is configured)

What may remain

  • Device-local data — saved games on this browser/device until you clear site data
  • Anonymous analytics — past events without email (if analytics was enabled)
  • Server logs — standard hosting logs for security for a limited operational period
  • Legal retention — records we must keep by law

Request deletion without the app

Open Dynasty Hockey and choose More → Support / Send Feedback if you cannot complete deletion from the app. We will verify ownership before completing deletion.

Partial failures

If cloud deletion fails, the app shows which step failed and whether you can retry. Contact support with your username — never send passwords or OAuth tokens.
